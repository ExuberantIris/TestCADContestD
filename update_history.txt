src/lp_mo_init.cpp — replaced the ad-hoc momentum/gradient heuristic with an exact algorithm: setup(SS) and hold(FF) decompose into independent problems (disjoint variables), each solved by bisecting the achievable WNS threshold and checking feasibility via SPFA negative-cycle detection on the tree's difference-constraint graph. src/main.cpp — added a safety net: since greedy's single-buffer hill-climbing is path-dependent, a better LP-init start doesn't always reach a better local optimum.
